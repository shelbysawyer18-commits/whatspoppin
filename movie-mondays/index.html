How it works

01. Make an account and submit a qualifying movie review during the entry period.

02. Entries are locked at the weekly deadline. One eligible person gets one entry, even with multiple reviews.

03. A server-side, auditable selection picks the winner automatically.

04. The result and verification details are published here. The winner receives private instructions to claim a ticket.

This is a visual preview. No entries are being collected, no drawing is scheduled, and no prizes are being awarded. Rules, eligibility, age/geographic restrictions, free-entry method, and ticket limits must be finalized before launch.

Transparency first.

The real launch should publish the entry cutoff, total eligible entries, anonymized participant commitments, the randomness source, the selection algorithm, and the timestamped outcome, without exposing users' private information.
